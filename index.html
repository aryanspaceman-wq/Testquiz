The science behind the buzz

BEYOND THE BUZZ

What really drives teenage energy drink consumption?

“Your age. Your habits. Your environment. Your feed. What influences the choices teenagers make?”

We're investigating energy drink consumption among teenagers in Qatar to understand the role of social influence, digital culture, everyday routines, and the local environment.

WHY THIS STUDY MATTERS

Anonymous research survey. No names, account handles, or private social media information required.

Estimated time: about 5 minutes.

Why this study matters

This project supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production. We look for associations between energy drink habits and things like age, area, friends, social media, routines and awareness. We do not assume any one of them causes consumption, and the results will not claim to represent all teenagers in Qatar.
